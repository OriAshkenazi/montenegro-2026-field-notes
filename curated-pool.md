# Curated activity schema

The full normalized activity pool is embedded in `itinerary.json` and rendered in the PWA’s Curated Experience Pool section. Each candidate contains: Item ID, Region, Activity or Venue, Interest Tag, Estimated Duration, Cash Required, Estimated Cost (EUR), Daylight Sensitive, Operating Status Verified, and an operating note.

The pool covers Coast, Central, and North regions and all requested interest tags. “Operating status verified” means an active published listing or official fee exists; it does not mean a date-specific reservation or October departure is confirmed. Follow linked sources and contact operators before travel.
