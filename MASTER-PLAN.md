# Montenegro master plan · Oct 1–6, 2026

For two adults, TLV–TIV, with two large checked cases and two cabin trolleys. Checked against current online information on Sep 26, 2026. Itinerary and budget are also available in the offline PWA at `index.html`.

## Executive mobility and financial architecture

**Vehicle:** reserve a guaranteed estate/wagon (Octavia Combi class or similar) or large crossover. A category label cannot guarantee four bags below a parcel shelf: require written model confirmation and a photo/measurement, then test the actual boot before signing. If it fails, insist on a replacement with verified covered luggage space. Higher ground clearance and sound tires are useful; 4×4 is not necessary on the planned paved route.

**Insurance:** request SCDW with low/zero excess, then explicit cover for glass, tires/wheels, underbody, gravel damage and roadside/towing. Read exclusions and police-report requirements. Enterprise’s terms list glass, wheels/tires and underside exclusions from its SCDW, so SCDW alone does not cover the risks requested. [Enterprise TIV terms](https://enterprise.me.enterprise.me/car-hire/locations/me/tivat-airport-tivt61)

**Airport buffers:** allow 60–90 minutes after landing for bags, counter, inspection, luggage test and setup; the supplied 15:15–15:30 start remains the schedule anchor. On Oct 6, leave Tivat by 09:30, return the car by 11:30 and reach the terminal no later than 12:15. Confirm rental shuttle/key-drop details before travel.

## Feasibility decision

With five nights, a Day 6 hotel within 30 minutes of TIV and a maximum of 3.5 hours after a +20% mountain allowance, it is not credible to include Žabljak/Durmitor and guarantee a return to Tivat on Day 5. The current route also cannot use Đurđevića Tara Bridge: the Transport Administration lists a full closure through Oct 26, 2026 at 12:00. The itinerary below is the safe, time-bounded coast + central route. It covers the Coast, Lovćen/Cetinje, and Lake Skadar, and represents all seven interest tags. See `curated-pool.md` for the northern item and status.

### Day-by-day parking and bag handling

Day 1: go directly to hotel after the rental handoff. Day 2 and Day 3: check in/unload before sightseeing. Day 4: accommodation first, boat second. Day 5: go directly to the Tivat hotel, unload, then spa. All lodging should confirm guarded or controlled parking when booked. No luggage stop is left in a vehicle during an excursion.

## Alpine weather branch

If P14/Sedlo, Durmitor approaches, or trails are compromised by freezing rain, early snow, fog, or a road restriction, **use the Coast + Central schedule above as the five-day fallback**: Kotor and Bay coast (Days 1–2), Lovćen/Cetinje (Day 3), Lake Skadar/Virpazar (Day 4), Tivat (Day 5). This retains sightseeing, food, hiking, wildlife/nature, spa, wine and romantic time without entering the alpine zone.

The app’s Alpine inclusion toggle shows a conditional north branch. It is not marked as passing the drive ceiling. If choosing it, add at least one day or agree to a longer drive day before booking. Check current AMSCG road conditions and the official 112 MNE app daily. Do not take the closed Đurđevića Tara Bridge route.

## Local PWA and deployment

`index.html`, `style.css`, `app.js`, `itinerary.json`, `manifest.webmanifest`, and `sw.js` form a dependency-free static PWA. The service worker precaches the complete shell and itinerary JSON cache-first; maps and official source links open online. Local storage remembers the cash reminder. Build is static: serve this folder from HTTPS or localhost and open `index.html`; no package compilation is required.

GitHub Pages was not deployed from this environment: the available `gh` account token is invalid, and the existing nested `agentic-trip-planner` Git repository belongs to a different remote and contains pre-existing user changes. This new self-contained project has no remote configured. After setting a destination repo and authenticating, deploy the contents of this folder to Pages; live URL is therefore **not yet available**.
