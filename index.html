        01 · Mobility

        Book a midsize estate by exact model.

        Request a Škoda Octavia Combi or larger estate, with written confirmation that two 23 kg checked suitcases and two cabin trolleys fit below a closed parcel shelf. Boot liters alone do not prove the bags fit: test-load all four before accepting the car.

        Ask for low-excess SCDW and separately listed glass, tires/wheels, underbody, gravel and roadside cover. Confirm rental road restrictions and avoid unpaved routes.

        02 · Road conditions

        Check before every mountain drive.

        Use AMSCG updates, the official 112 MNE channel and your lodging host. Durmitor may see frost or snow in October. P14 is the featured route only when Sedlo is open, clear and safe; cancel for snow, ice, poor visibility or unsafe conditions. Check the complete out-and-back with waypoints and +20% terrain time before departure; drop detours or switch branches if it exceeds 5.5 hours. The direct Day 1 transfer is the accepted after-sunset exception described in the primary route. On other mountain days, finish high-altitude driving by 16:00.

        Đurđevića Tara Bridge is officially closed 10 Aug–26 Oct 2026 at 12:00. The itinerary does not cross or depend on the bridge.

        03 · Emergency

        Save these numbers.

        The offline list includes AMSCG roadside assistance and Mountain Rescue Service. Confirm your embassy’s current consular emergency number before travel.

        04 · Bags & parking

        Check in before exploring.

        Never leave luggage visible or unattended in the car. On transfer days, go straight to the booked lodging, unload and check in, then head out. Reserve guarded parking in both Kotor and Žabljak.

        Allow 60–90 minutes at TIV for rental pickup. On Oct 6, return the car by 11:30 and reach the terminal by 12:15.
