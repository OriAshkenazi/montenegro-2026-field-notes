Useful before you set out.

01 · Mobility

Book the boot, not the class.

Request an estate/wagon or larger crossover and written confirmation that 2 checked cases + 2 cabin trolleys fit beneath a closed parcel shelf. Do a physical fit check before accepting the keys.

Ask for SCDW with low excess plus explicit glass, tires/wheels, underbody, gravel and roadside cover. SCDW alone commonly excludes glass, tires and underside damage.

02 · Road conditions

Autumn changes quickly.

Use the official 112 MNE app and AMSCG road updates each morning. Durmitor’s high routes may see frost or snow; confirm P14/Sedlo conditions before departure. Do not rely on a route planner to confirm a pass is open.

Đurđevića Tara Bridge closure: 10 Aug–26 Oct 2026, 12:00. Keep it out of all routes during this trip.

04 · Bags & parking

Check in before exploring.

Never leave luggage visible or unattended in the car. On transfer days, go straight to lodging or guarded parking, unload and check in, then head out. Use hotel or marked public lots in Kotor and Virpazar.

Allow 60–90 minutes at TIV for the rental pickup. On Oct 6, return the car by 11:30 and reach the terminal by 12:15.
